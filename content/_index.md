Solo tech consulting. Continuous discovery, design, and delivery — one engagement, end to end.

## What I do

I help small product teams ship software that people actually use. I work solo, embed with your team for the shape of an engagement, and run discovery, design, and delivery as one continuous loop instead of three hand-offs.

## The triple diamond

Each diamond runs on its own clock, but they overlap. The point of running them together is to keep decisions close to the work and to keep the work close to the user.

## How engagements work

- **Fixed scope** — when the problem is well-defined and the answer is mostly known
- **Retainer** — when you need a steady delivery partner for a quarter or more
- **Hourly** — when the work is open-ended and you want someone senior on call

First conversation is short, free, and ends with one of three outcomes: a clear next step, a polite no, or a referral to someone better suited.
